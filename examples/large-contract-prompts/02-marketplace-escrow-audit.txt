Type of work: Solidity security audit · enhanced

Perform a defensive security audit of a multi-contract Solidity 0.8.20
marketplace consisting of:

- Marketplace: listing creation, purchase, cancellation, fee accounting.
- Escrow: seller proceeds, buyer disputes, refunds, pull-payment withdrawal.
- FeePolicy: bounded fee updates controlled by a two-step administrator.

The complete submitted source should contain at least twelve named functions
and all three contracts. Review every constructor, receive/fallback function,
and named function. Build an actor and permission matrix, trace ETH through
every state transition, identify cross-contract trust assumptions, rank
findings by severity, describe exploit preconditions, and propose precise
repairs and regression tests.

Pay special attention to stale listings, replay, double settlement, fee bounds,
reentrancy, failed recipients, accounting conservation, denial of service,
front-running, and administrator transitions.

Do not deploy, sign, request secrets, or call the result a formal audit.
